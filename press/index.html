Long — full bio

Vipul Kella, MD has spent nineteen years practicing emergency medicine while building a parallel career in health-tech investing and operating. Rather than leaving clinical practice behind, he expanded where his clinical lens gets applied — into deal flow, company diligence, and event curation.

He founded Health Nexus Partners on a simple observation: healthcare's biggest decisions happen between the wrong people. Physicians rarely get access to capital networks. Investors rarely get a clinical reality check before writing a check. Policymakers sit outside both conversations. Health Nexus Partners exists to put physicians, investors, founders, and policymakers in the same room — through curated dinners, flagship conferences like Women's Health Nexus, and SPVs vetted through direct clinical and operating experience.

Vipul speaks and writes from the intersection of three lenses: clinical ("does this change what I do with a patient?"), capital ("is money flowing to the right problem?"), and operator ("who needs to be in the room for this to move?"). He continues to practice emergency medicine.
