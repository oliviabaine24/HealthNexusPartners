About

Vipul Kella, MD

Emergency Physician · Investor · Founder, Health Nexus Partners

"Healthcare doesn't have an innovation problem. It has a translation problem. The clinical knowledge, the capital, and the policy intent already exist — they just sit in separate rooms."

Speaking Topics Press & Media Kit

The physician who stayed in the fight.

Vipul has spent nineteen years practicing emergency medicine while building a parallel career in health-tech investing and operating. He didn't leave medicine to do this work — he expanded where his clinical lens gets applied.

He sits where physicians rarely do: inside the deal flow. As an active investor, he's seen what gets funded and what should get funded, and the gap between those two lists is most of what's wrong with healthcare capital today. As a practicing ER physician, he knows which products change what happens with a patient and which ones are one more thing to ignore at 3 AM.

That combination — clinical practice, capital, and operating — is why he founded Health Nexus Partners: to put the people who need each other in the same room, on purpose, instead of leaving it to chance.

The lens behind everything he says.
