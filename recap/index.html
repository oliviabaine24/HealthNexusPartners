Women's Health Nexus 2026 · Recap

175 people in the room. One conversation nobody else was having.

Physicians, founders, and investors spent a full day at the Congressional Country Club working through the real economics of women's health — cardiovascular care, precision oncology, diagnostic infrastructure, and the policy fights behind all of it. Here's what happened.

The lineup

Five sessions that shaped the day

Cardiovascular Innovation
Why women's heart disease still gets misread as "atypical," and what's finally starting to change at the bedside and in reimbursement.
Precision Oncology & Economics
Reimbursement, access, and the real wedge left in a category that's already "mature" — plus a hard look at how the category talks about itself.
Diagnostic Infrastructure Roundtable
Interoperability, policy incentives, and why the patient — not the EMR — is the only consistent thread through the system.
Leadership & Executive Panel
AI, policy momentum in 25 states, and the data point that should reframe the whole conversation: women with two children report 4% of a day for self-care.
Pitch Competition
Ten vetted founders, seven judges, one winner — judged on problem and impact, solution and edge, business model, traction, and team.
Pitch Competition Winner

Brainify AI, led by CEO Miriam Keretynova, took the top spot — earning roughly $50,000 in in-kind services and 90 days of curated introductions on the investor and go-to-market side.
